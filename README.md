# hand-gesture-synth
A synth that allows you to play chords using hand gestures. Control the chord, root, volume, and inversion, allowing expressive playing.

The original hand recognition model comes from https://github.com/kinivi/hand-gesture-recognition-mediapipe

Control change through chord inversions a, b, and c by rotating the left hand inwards.
Vary volume by rotating the right hand inwards for quieter and outwards for louder.
Control chords by displaying hand gestures in the left hand, and change the root note with gestures in the right hand.
Vary the octave by raising or lowering the right hand.

Try exploring the gestures by counting on your fingers. Begin from your thumb, from your pinky, and from your index to show 12 chords and all 12 notes of the chromatic scale.
